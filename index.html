How I run a deal

Forecasts are only as good as the evidence behind them. Three habits keep mine honest.

Qualify weekly

Every opportunity is checked against MEDDPICC each week: metrics, economic buyer, criteria, process, paper, pain, champion and competition.

  • Named economic buyer
  • Quantified pain
  • Zero forecast slippage for eight quarters

Plan it together

A mutual action plan turns “us selling to them” into a shared project with dates, owners and legal timelines agreed up front.

  • Security review runs in parallel
  • Redline turnarounds scheduled
  • 10+ stakeholders mapped per account

Defend the price

I build the financial case before the proposal. If procurement wants concessions, I trade for term, upfront payment or a case study, never for list price.

  • Payback under nine months
  • No discount on 85% of deals
  • 135% net revenue retention
