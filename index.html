The Origin Story

From 90 Cold Calls a Day to 8-Figure Enterprise Deals

I didn't start with an enterprise rolodex or inbound leads. I cut my teeth in B2B tech the hardest way possible: cold outbound prospecting as an SDR, grinding through 90 dials and personalized emails every single day.

That experience taught me the foundational rule of revenue: active listening always beats smooth talking. An executive will tell you their deepest organizational headaches if they sense you actually care about solving their problem rather than hitting your month-end quota. That SDR resilience still powers how I run enterprise territories today.

My Closing Philosophy

Why I Refuse Discounting & 'Hope-Based' Selling

When an enterprise sales rep folds and offers a 35% discount on the 29th of the month, they signal to procurement that their software was never worth full price in the first place. Worse, they destroy company gross margins.

My philosophy is rooted in defensible CFO value engineering. Before I ever present a commercial proposal, my team and I have already quantified the cost of downtime, the headcount hours reclaimed, and the regulatory risk eliminated. When the numbers show a 6.8-month payback, price ceases to be a hurdle.

Enterprise Execution Playbook

The Operating System for Consistent 150%+ Attainment

How I manage enterprise pipelines from territory mapping to signature — replacing guesswork with verifiable governance.

The 8 Pillars of MEDDPICC Qualification

Every pipeline opportunity is pressure-tested weekly against these 8 objective verification points:

M
Metrics

Quantified economic impact ($ Saved, hours reclaimed, revenue accelerated).

E
Economic Buyer

Direct access to the individual with P&L authority and veto power.

D
Decision Criteria

Formal technical, security, financial, and operational scoring rubrics.

D
Decision Process

Exact committee workflow, timeline, and stakeholder sign-off stages.

P
Paper Process

Legal, infosec questionnaires, MSA review, DPA clauses, and vendor onboarding.

I
Identify Pain

Deep organizational distress causing tangible executive urgency.

C
Champion

Internal sponsor with political capital actively selling on our behalf.

C
Competition

Competitive alternatives, internal build attempts, and the cost of "Do Nothing".

Mutual Action Plans (MAP): Ending Quarter-End Surprises

A MAP shifts the dynamic from "us selling to them" into "joint project management towards buyer value realization":

Phase 1: Discovery & Criteria

Establish shared business objectives, quantify pain, and gain mutual agreement on success criteria for the evaluation.

Phase 2: Tech Eval & Security

Time-boxed 14-day technical proof, concurrent Infosec/SOC2 questionnaire submission, and architecture review board.

Phase 3: Procurement & MSA

Scheduled legal redline turnarounds, pre-aligned commercial terms with Economic Buyer, and PO issuance governance.

Multi-Threading: Mapping the Enterprise Matrix

In complex 7-figure sales, an average of 11.4 decision makers participate. I orchestrate all five key personas:

1. The Economic Buyer (P&L Holder)
CFO / CIO / SVP of Engineering

Care about: Risk mitigation, business continuity, ROI, and EBITDA impact. I present customized financial models, not product decks.

2. The Internal Champion
VP of Platform / Chief Architect / Director

Care about: Career success and team efficiency. I arm them with internal slide kits, competitor teardowns, and leadership memos.

3. Technical Evaluator (The Gatekeeper)
Lead Staff Engineer / SecOps / CISO

Care about: Architectural purity, latency, API documentation, and zero false positives. I partner with Sales Engineering for deep dives.

4. Legal & Procurement
Chief Procurement Officer / Enterprise Counsel

Care about: Indemnification, liability limits, and SLA guarantees. I negotiate value trades rather than slashing baseline contract ACV.

CFO Value Engineering: Eliminating Price Resistance

When buyers request a 30% discount, reps without a business case surrender margin. Here is how I defend contract pricing:

Hardware & Tool Consolidation

Map redundant legacy licenses (e.g. replacing 4 disjointed point solutions) to generate self-funding ROI within 90 days of kickoff.

Revenue Acceleration Modeling

Demonstrate that a 3-week acceleration in deployment time captures $2.4M in incremental client billing, dwarfing the software ACV.

Trade Value, Never Price

If procurement demands pricing concessions, trade solely for multi-year commitments, upfront annual payment, or public case studies.
